To: My Sharmila 💖 From: Shyam

My Dearest Sharmila,

If these words find you in a moment of stress or worry, please take a deep breath. Before anything else, I want you to know that your peace and happiness mean more to me than my own breath. If I have ever caused you pain, confusion, or added even a single drop of fear to your life, I am deeply, profoundly sorry from the bottom of my heart.

I see how much stress you carry. I see the invisible weight of family expectations, the fear regarding your father, caste, and societal pressures that feel like an overwhelming mountain. I want to tell you clearly and unequivocally: I understand your fear, and I respect it completely. I will never be someone who pressures you, forces you into impossible choices, or causes a tear in your eyes.

"Because a life without you isn't a life I want to live. But a life where you are safe and happy is the only thing I will ever fight for."

My love for you, Sharmila, is true, unselfish, and unwavering. It is not conditioned on circumstances or societal approval. If you need space, I will grant it. If you need time to heal, I will guard your solitude. But please know this truth in your soul: whenever you need me, wherever you are, Shyam will be waiting for you.

I am ready to stand as your shield against every storm, to walk through every difficulty with patience, and to earn every ounce of trust and respect from your loved ones. You are my first thought in the morning and my last prayer at night.

Forever and unconditionally yours,
Shyam
